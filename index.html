CORE VALUES

01
First Principle

DISCIPLINE

Every champion is built on a foundation of discipline. At UMF, we don't just train bodies — we train minds. Showing up every day, pushing past comfort, and respecting the process is what separates a student from a warrior.

Daily Practice Mental Toughness Consistency
🧠
02
Second Principle

STRENGTH

Strength is more than muscle. At UMF, we build physical power through MMA, Boxing, CrossFit and more — but the real strength we develop is the kind that carries you through life's toughest moments.

Physical Power Inner Resilience Combat Fitness
💪
03
Third Principle

FOCUS

In the ring and in life, focus is everything. Our training sharpens your attention, reaction time, and situational awareness. A focused mind is the most dangerous weapon any martial artist can have.

Sharp Mind Reaction Speed Awareness
